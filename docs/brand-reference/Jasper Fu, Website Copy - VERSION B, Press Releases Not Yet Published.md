## **0.2 Design Instructions** [***Source: Jasper Fu Visual Identity***](https://canva.link/7yc9v8nlxdw3s02)

### **Color Palette**

| Role | Value | Usage |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Ink Navy, primary | `#0A1628` | Backgrounds, wordmark, dominant surface color |
| Signal Bronze, accent | `#B4905A` | Rules, monogram ring, pull-quotes, callouts only, one per composition |
| Graphite, secondary | `#3A4453` | Body copy on light backgrounds, captions, UI chrome |
| Bone, paper | `#F5F1E8` | Primary light background |
| Settlement Blue, tertiary | `#3A5C9E` | Charts, links, data highlights only, never adjacent to Bronze |

Navy and Bone carry roughly 80 percent of any page. Bronze appears once per composition, never as a fill. Never navy text on graphite, never bronze text on bone.

### **Typography**

* Display serif (headlines, name lockup, pull-quotes): GT Sectra Display, Canela, or Freight Display; Georgia as the fallback  
* Body sans (text, captions, data, UI): Söhne, Inter, or Neue Haas Grotesk; Calibri as the fallback  
* Section labels and eyebrows: sans-serif, uppercase, \+30 tracking, bronze or navy only

### **Layout Patterns**

* Eyebrow label, then serif headline, then sans supporting copy, repeated at the top of every section  
* Two-column layouts pairing a label list with explanatory copy  
* Stat cards: solid Navy block for the primary metric, Bone cards for supporting context  
* Quote cards: single bronze left-rule, one quote per card  
* Proof points: bronze checkmarks, never plain bullets, used sparingly

### **Imagery**

* Editorial financial-press photography, not startup marketing: still, direct, engineered  
* The NYSE still is the benchmark asset for framing, grading, and wardrobe  
* Headshots: formal color and editorial black and white, hi-res (300dpi print) and web (72dpi) resolution  
* Avoid crypto-visual clichés, ring-light or phone-camera candids, and busy or branded backdrops

### **Tone**

Confident operator, proof-backed, independent, institutional, security-first. Ink Navy and Bone dominate; Bronze appears once per composition and never competes with Settlement Blue on the same element.

### **Logo and Lockup Rules**

The primary mark for press use is the "JASPER FU" wordmark in the display serif, all caps, wide tracking. The JF monogram, Ink Navy field, Bone lettering, single Bronze ring, is the secondary mark. Never recolor, stretch, rotate, or add effects to either mark.
